Persona 4: Negative persona: Samir, The Unauthorized Reseller ("Courtier")

Demographic info
NameSamir
Age45
OccupationSecurity Agent (Single)
Tech skill levelLow (Almost literate; relies on images, icons, and voice messages, struggles with dense text)
Goals
Goals1- Browse featured promotions and swipeable car cards to spot high-demand vehicles to resell as an unofficial middleman.
2- Extract high-quality photos and basic pricing from car description pages to repost on external platforms (Facebook Marketplace, Ouedkniss) with an added commission.
3- Schedule visits and test drives to inspect the cars and capture live photos and videos to convince external buyers.
Pain points
Pain points1- Confused by dense text, technical specification tables (engine type, horsepower, safety features), and multi-step filter forms.
2- Frustrated by showroom image watermarks and copyright protections that make copying listings difficult.
3- Blocked by mandatory identity pre-qualification (driver's license upload and deposit hold) when attempting to schedule visits or test drives.
Expected interaction
Expected interactionSamir uses the mobile app exclusively for visual browsing via swipeable cards, completely skipping text filters and specification tables. He saves vehicles to his wishlist to track new arrivals and price changes, takes screenshots of car description galleries to create unofficial listings on Facebook and Ouedkniss, and initiates in-app messaging with sales representatives to book test drives for photo opportunities or negotiate informal broker cuts without intending to buy.

Scenario 4: Negative persona

Samir starts by opening the mobile app to browse new arrivals using the swipeable cards, skipping written search filters and focusing entirely on vehicle images. On the car description page, he screenshots the high-quality photos and saves the car to his wishlist to track promotional price changes, then immediately reposts the vehicle on Ouedkniss and Facebook Marketplace with a personal markup. When an external buyer calls him asking for live video proof, Samir uses the in-app messaging feature to schedule a showroom visit and test drive, aiming to test the car and record live walk-around videos to close his resale. However, when the app prompts him with a required verification step to upload a valid driver's license and pre-authorize a deposit before the sales representative confirms the booking, Samir feels overwhelmed by the written requirements and cancels the request.
