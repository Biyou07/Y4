wireframe (mid fidelity) is the best fit.

unclear.

Prototyping

--- Slide 55 ---
AI layouts gave diverse alternatives and a new idea
(users build filters dynamically by choosing fields,
operators, and values).
Results
Weaknesses: repeated prompting in one session led to
cumulative errors, and code generated from screenshots
reproduced the visuals but not the interactions.
Interactive prototypes improved feedback: users held
back detailed critique on sketches and low-fidelity
designs but engaged and found issues with interactive
ones.

--- Slide 56 ---
Conclusions
Generative UI speeds up communication and feedback
but brings code errors, security concerns, and a risk of
converging on conventional patterns. When to introduce
AI
depends
on
the
team’s
expertise,
and
prompt-engineering skill and domain knowledge are
needed.

--- Slide 59 ---
1.
Design → Prototype → Evaluate → Refine
The goal is not to create a perfect prototype immediately
but to progressively improve the design based on user
feedback.
2.
Key
Takeaways
Whether we create prototypes manually or using AI
tools, the objective remains the same: to design
solutions that meet users' needs. AI can accelerate
the process, but it cannot replace the importance of
understanding users and evaluating usability.
