Human-Computer Interaction Definitions
As a field of research, HCI is situated at the intersection of
computer science, behavioral sciences, design, media studies, and
several other fields of study. The term was popularized by Stuart
K. Card, Allen Newell, and Thomas P. Moran in their 1983 book, The
Psychology of Human–Computer Interaction

--- Slide 15 ---
Human-Computer Interaction Definitions
HCI is research in the design and the use of computer technology,
which focuses on the interfaces between people (users) and
computers. HCI researchers observe the ways humans interact with
computers and design technologies that allow humans to interact
with computers in novel ways

What are the human factors?
The scientific study of how humans interact with
systems, focused on human capabilities,
limitations, and characteristics, is used to
design safer, more efficient, and more comfortable
systems.

--- Slide 22 ---
●
●

UI (User Interface): the visual and interactive surface of a
product: buttons, screens, layout, motion, and feedback. What
the user directly touches or sees.
UX (User Experience): the full journey of using a product: ease
of navigation, accessibility, and how it feels to accomplish a
task from start to finish. UI is one layer inside UX.
CX (Customer Experience): every interaction a customer has with
a brand, not just the product: marketing, sales, customer
service, brand perception. UX is one part of CX.

Usability as
Competitive Value

--- Slide 26 ---
Once two competing products offer the same functionality at
the same price, we will have that: price and features stop
being differentiators.
Usability: the extent to which a product enables users to
achieve their goals effectively, efficiently, and with
satisfaction. It is measured not by what a system can
technically do, but by how easily, quickly, and pleasantly a
human can actually do it.

--- Slide 49 ---
WHY WE FOCUS ON USER & INTERACTION?
For decades, computing was machine-centered:
Old paradigm: "What can computers do?"
Humans adapted to the machine; success meant processing power and
features.

--- Slide 50 ---
WHY WE FOCUS ON USER & INTERACTION?
A New Understanding of Computing
Ben Shneiderman's Leonardo's Laptop (2002) reframed this into a
human-centered paradigm:
New paradigm: "What can humans do with computers?"
This shift is the conceptual core of HCI: success isn't what a machine
can execute, but what it enables a person to accomplish, understand,
or create.
