Deep Learning Lecture 1: Core Fundamentals
==========================================

Key Concepts:
1. Artificial Neural Networks: Perceptrons, Multi-Layer Perceptrons (MLPs), and Activation Functions (ReLU, GELU, Sigmoid).
2. Optimization: Gradient Descent, Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD), Adam, and Learning Rate Schedules.
3. Loss Functions: Cross-Entropy Loss for Classification, Mean Squared Error (MSE) for Regression.
4. Generalization & Regularization: Dropout, Weight Decay (L2), Batch Normalization, and Early Stopping.

Exam Review Questions:
- Why does the vanishing gradient problem occur in deep networks using Sigmoid?
- How does Adam optimizer combine Momentum and RMSprop?
- Explain the role of the covariance matrix in PCA dimension reduction.
