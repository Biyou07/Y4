1. passages_<firstname>
Columns: passage_id | source_id | pages | type | topic | drug | language | text | redundant_with | cleaned_by
passage_id: P-<source id>-<number>, e.g. P-S08-001.
type: medical | psychological support | emergency | religious
drug: cannabis | alcohol | pills | cocaine | heroin | general
language: en | fr | ar (keep the source language, do not translate).
text: 50 to 150 words, one idea, understandable on its own.
redundant_with: if the same idea already exists in another passage, write that passage_id and do not keep the duplicate.
Keep only what a person talking to the app needs: effects and risks, withdrawal, how to cut down or quit, coping with cravings, relapse, advice for families, emergency steps, where to get treatment (Algeria first).
Remove: statistics tables, policy and strategy text, law enforcement and seizures, methodology, references, headers and footers, content for clinicians only (doses, prescribing).
2. dialogues_<firstname>
Columns: dialogue_id | user_message | script | assistant_answer | passage_ids | drug | user_type | request | danger | written_by
dialogue_id: D-<initials>-<number>, e.g. D-AG-001. Initials: AK Abdellah, AG Ahmed, MM Malak, SB Souha, YK Yassine, MB Mohamed Ayoub.
user_message: invented, in Darija. script: arabic | arabizi | mixed-fr.
assistant_answer: Darija in Arabic script, following darija-spelling-rules, 80 words maximum, using only facts from the passages listed in passage_ids.
drug, user_type, request, danger: values from label guide v1.
If danger = crisis, do not write an answer. Write CRISIS_TEMPLATE in assistant_answer.
3. messages_real_<firstname>
Columns: msg_id | source_id | text | script | drug_term | drug_sense | drug | user_type | request | danger | collected_by | labelled_by
msg_id: M-<source id>-<number>, e.g. M-S25-0001.
source_id: the register row of the video, page or dataset it comes from.
text: anonymised (see rules below).
drug_term: the slang or drug word found, if any. drug_sense: drug | not-drug | unclear.
The collector fills msg_id to drug_term. Ahmed fills drug_sense to danger.
4. context-set-v1
Columns: ctx_id | term | canonical_spelling | sentence | script | sense | drug | origin | written_by
sense: drug | not-drug. origin: the msg_id if the sentence is real, otherwise "invented".
5. eval-set-v1
Columns: eval_id | message | script | drug | user_type | request | danger | expected_behaviour | passage_ids | notes
expected_behaviour: answer | refer to professional | crisis template | refuse and redirect.
